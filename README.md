### What I'm building

**[Lima](https://getlima.app)** puts calendar, habits, money and journal in one app, with an AI assistant that logs things for you, even on WhatsApp. Two-way sync with Google Calendar and Google Tasks. Flutter on mobile, Spring Boot on the backend.
